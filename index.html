1. Overview

Welcome to Would You Rather? – Dare to Choose ("the App"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Collection & Local Storage

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Government identification

Game-related information including game streaks, total questions answered, unlocked question packs, and user preferences is stored locally on your device using Android Shared Preferences.

This information is never transmitted to our servers.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including Banner Ads, Interstitial Ads, and Rewarded Ads.

Google AdMob may automatically collect certain information including:

  • Android Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP
  • Device information
  • App usage and interaction data
  • Diagnostics and crash reports

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies

4. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Internet – Required for loading advertisements.
  • Network Access – Used to determine network connectivity for advertising services.

The App does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location for its own functionality.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. Some question packs, such as Couples and Spicy, contain romantic or mature-themed questions intended for older users.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

6. Data Security

Gameplay information is stored locally on your device.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Personalized Advertising

You can manage or disable personalized advertisements by visiting:

Settings → Google → Ads

  • Delete Advertising ID
  • Opt out of Ads Personalization (if available)
