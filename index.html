Privacy Policy

Would You Rather? – Dare to Choose

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to Would You Rather? – Dare to Choose ("the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our policies and procedures regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information Collection & Local Storage

The App does not collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address).

All game data—including your game streaks, total questions answered, and unlocked question packs—is stored locally on your device using shared preferences and is never transmitted to our servers.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

This App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads) for monetization.

AdMob may collect and use certain data to provide personalized and non-personalized advertising, including:

  • Device advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and general location data
  • App interaction and usage metrics
  • Diagnostics and crash logs

For detailed information on how Google collects and processes ad data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Policies.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our App includes content tailored for different audiences. The Kids Safe pack is suitable for general audiences. However, packs such as Spicy and Couples contain romantic/flirtatious themes intended for teens and adults.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

5. Opt-Out of Personalized Ads

You can manage or opt out of personalized interest-based advertising directly on your Android device:

  • Go to Settings > Google > Ads
  • Tap Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
